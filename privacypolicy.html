PRIVACY POLICY Phishing Email Analyzer (Chrome extension) Last updated: October 17, 2026 IN SHORT The extension reads an email only when you ask it to, analyzes it on your device, and does not collect, store, sell, or send your email data to anyone. WHAT THE EXTENSION DOES Phishing Email Analyzer helps you decide whether an email may be a phishing attempt. You can click a button to analyze the email you have open in Gmail, or you can paste in an email's source or open a saved .eml file. The extension scores the email for common warning signs and shows you the results in its popup. INFORMATION THE EXTENSION HANDLES To do its job, the extension handles the content of the email you choose to analyze. This can include the sender, Reply-To, and Return-Path addresses and other email headers, the text of the message, and the links in the message. This happens only when you click an analyze button, or when you paste or open an email yourself. The extension does not read your email in the background. HOW THE INFORMATION IS USED The email content is used for one purpose only: to calculate the phishing score and show you the reasons for it. All analysis runs locally in your browser. WHAT WE COLLECT, STORE, AND SHARE We do not collect your data. The developer has no server, database, or account system, and receives no information from the extension. We do not store your data. Email content exists only while the popup is open. The extension does not save it, and it is gone when you close the popup. We do not share or sell your data. Nothing is sent to third parties, analytics services, or advertisers. The extension has no analytics, tracking, or cookies. One note on how Gmail analysis works: to read the full original message, the extension asks Gmail for it using your existing signed-in session, the same way Gmail's own "Download message" feature does. That request goes to Google's Gmail service, not to the developer, and is subject to Google's own privacy policy. PERMISSIONS The extension needs permission to run in your current Gmail tab. This is used only after you click "Analyze the open Gmail email," to read the message you have open. The extension requests no other permissions and loads no code from outside the extension package. LIMITED USE DISCLOSURE The use of information received by this extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Specifically: - Data is used only to provide the extension's single purpose, which is analyzing an email for phishing signs. - Data is not transferred to others, except as needed to comply with applicable law. - Data is never used or transferred for personalized, re-targeted, or interest-based advertising. - Data is never read by humans, because the developer never receives it. - Data is not used or transferred to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes. SECURITY AND LIMITS OF THE TOOL Because nothing leaves your device, there is no stored data on our side to protect or leak. The extension is a screening aid and not a guarantee of safety. A low score does not mean an email is safe, and a high score does not prove it is malicious. Always verify the sender before clicking links or sharing information.
